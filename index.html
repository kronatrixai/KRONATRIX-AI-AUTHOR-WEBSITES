Why this matters now

Search is becoming a conversation.

Readers no longer have to type short keywords and open ten tabs. They can ask a full question, describe exactly what they want and ask an AI system to help them choose.

The risk for older author websites

Many author websites were built before AI-assisted search became a major discovery surface.

If the book, genre, audience, proof, preview and next action are unclear or scattered, AI systems have less useful public information to work with. That can mean missed discovery—and missed sales opportunities—when a reader is actively looking for something to read.
