Quick answers

Common questions.

Can KRONATRIX guarantee AI recommendations or Google rankings?

No. KRONATRIX improves controllable factors such as clarity, crawlability, content, proof, internal links, structured data, image context and conversion paths. Search engines and AI systems decide what they index, rank, cite or recommend.

Why does a book preview website show less than 10%?

The service is designed to give readers a meaningful sample while keeping the vast majority of the book for the full purchase. The exact excerpt used is agreed for the project.

Are the £500 website packages monthly?

No. The standard book preview website and author website are £500 one-time services with no monthly KRONATRIX hosting fee and no hidden recurring KRONATRIX website charge. Optional domain or third-party costs are separate if requested.
