How to market my book: build demand before you ask for the sale.

Good book marketing is not one tactic. It is a connected journey from discovery to interest to preview to purchase—and then back to the author for the next book.

Build My Book DestinationSee the £500 Book Preview Website
The simple plan

Use every channel. Depend on none of them completely.

Social media, Amazon, interviews, advertising, Google and AI search can all help. The mistake is letting each channel tell a different story with no owned destination joining them together.

1. Make the book easy to explain

State the genre, reader, core promise, themes and reason someone should care in plain language.

2. Give readers a preview

Let an interested person experience a controlled sample before asking them to purchase the full book.

3. Build proof

Connect reviews, author identity, official social profiles and credible public information.

4. Make discovery possible in more places

Use strong metadata, real images, crawlable content and clear internal links so Google and AI-assisted search have useful public information to retrieve.

5. Send qualified interest to the retailer

Once the reader understands the book and wants more, make the Amazon or retailer purchase action obvious.

6. Keep the relationship

Give readers a way to follow the author or join an email list so the next launch does not start from zero.

Modern discovery

Book marketing now includes AI-assisted search.

Readers can ask detailed questions in ChatGPT, Google and Gemini instead of only browsing categories. The clearer your public book information is, the more useful source material exists when those systems answer relevant questions.

Early-mover idea

Build the author, book, image, review and backlink signals now—before AI-first book discovery becomes normal marketing practice.

Questions

How to market my book: quick answers.

What is the best way to market my book?

Use several channels, but give them one clear destination. A strong book page can explain the promise, show a preview, build trust and send the reader to buy, follow or join your email list.

Do I still need Amazon if I have a book website?

Yes. The website and Amazon do different jobs. The website can build interest and context; Amazon or another retailer can complete the purchase.

Can AI help readers discover my book?

AI-assisted search can surface public web information when it is relevant. Clear, crawlable book and author information can improve the material available to those systems, but no placement or recommendation is guaranteed.
