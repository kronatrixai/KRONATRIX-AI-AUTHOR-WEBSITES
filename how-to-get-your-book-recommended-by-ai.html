Practical Author Guide

How to make your book more recommendable by AI—without pretending it can be guaranteed.

The goal is to give AI-assisted search systems better public evidence to retrieve when a reader asks a relevant question. That means useful content, clear book/author relationships, real proof and strong technical accessibility.

Short answer

You cannot force an AI assistant to recommend your book. You can improve the conditions by publishing a useful official source about the book, connecting it clearly to the author, answering relevant reader questions, earning genuine third-party evidence and keeping the pages easy to crawl and index.

Seven-step framework

What to build around one book.

Create a dedicated official destination

Give the book a page or preview site with one canonical URL that can become the clearest owned source about that title.

Explain the reader fit

Describe the themes, genre/context and kind of reader who may appreciate the book. Do not turn this into a keyword list.

Offer genuine evidence

A controlled preview, real reviews, author information and live purchase links are stronger than unsupported claims about quality.

Connect the author entity

Link the book to the official author website and profiles so the relationship is consistent across public sources.

Answer related questions

Useful FAQs can cover where to preview the book, where to buy it, what it is about and how to follow the author.

Earn corroboration

Real reviews, client credits, interviews, guest features and genuine reader discussions strengthen the wider evidence graph.

Measure and improve

Track Search Console impressions, image visibility, AI-feature impressions where available, referral traffic and conversion actions.
