The sales journey

Do not ask a cold reader to buy before they understand why the book is for them.

A dedicated book-preview website can sit between discovery and Amazon. It gives the reader context, a sample and a reason to continue.

Cold traffic

Post → Amazon → hope

  • The reader may know almost nothing about the book.
  • The retailer page has to create all the desire.
  • The author has limited space to tell the wider story.
  • The relationship may end after the click.
Qualified traffic

Discover → Preview → Want → Buy

  • Explain exactly who the book is for.
  • Let the reader experience a small sample.
  • Show reviews, author identity and proof.
  • Make the correct Amazon purchase button obvious.
  • Keep a pathway to follow the author afterwards.
Five practical improvements

Increase the quality of the traffic you send to Amazon.

Clarify the promise

Tell the reader what kind of experience the book offers and who is most likely to enjoy it.

Use a controlled preview

Let the story do some of the selling before the purchase decision.

Show real proof

Use genuine reviews, author profiles and live evidence rather than vague marketing claims.

Build discoverability

Make the book understandable in Google Search, Google Images and AI-assisted discovery.

Make Amazon the obvious next step

Use a clear purchase button once the reader has enough information to act.

Build the next sale too

Give readers a way to follow the author or join the mailing list after they discover the book.

Questions

How to get more book sales on Amazon: quick answers.

How can I get more book sales on Amazon?

Create more qualified demand before the Amazon click. Help the reader understand the book, see proof and sample the story, then send interested readers to the correct Amazon listing.

Should I send every ad directly to Amazon?

Not always. A dedicated book page can be useful when the reader needs more context, a preview or author proof before buying. The right route depends on the campaign and audience.

Does a book website guarantee higher Amazon sales?

No. It can improve the pre-purchase journey, but sales also depend on the book, cover, pricing, reviews, audience, traffic and retailer listing.
