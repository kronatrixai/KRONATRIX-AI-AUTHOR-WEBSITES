Privacy & Analytics

Simple privacy information for enquiries and analytics.

This page explains what KRONATRIX for Authors collects through the qualification form, how Google Analytics is handled, and how to contact KRONATRIX about your data.

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Who is responsible for this website?

KRONATRIX operates this author-services website. Privacy enquiries can be sent to kronatrix.ai@gmail.com.

Qualification enquiries

The qualification form asks only for your email address. The service you selected is recorded with the form so KRONATRIX knows which service you are enquiring about. KRONATRIX uses this information to review the enquiry, contact you about suitability and explain relevant next steps.

The form is processed through Formspree. This means the information you submit is transmitted through Formspree's form-processing infrastructure so it can reach KRONATRIX.

Google Analytics

This website is configured to use Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-HVE7XRLZEP to understand website usage and improve the service. Analytics is treated as optional on this site: the Google Analytics tag is not loaded until you choose “Accept analytics” in the cookie banner.

If you decline analytics, the site remains usable and the Analytics tag is not loaded. Your email address from the qualification form is not sent to Google Analytics.

Cookie and consent preference

Your analytics choice is stored locally in your browser so the website can remember whether you accepted or declined. You can reopen the choice at any time using the link in the footer.

What KRONATRIX does not do with enquiry data

  • KRONATRIX does not send the email address entered in the qualification form to Google Analytics.
  • KRONATRIX does not sell qualification enquiry data to advertisers.
  • KRONATRIX does not ask for passwords, payment-card details or manuscript files in the initial qualification form.

How long information is kept

Enquiry information is kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond to the enquiry, manage any resulting client relationship, maintain appropriate business records, or meet applicable legal obligations. Information that is no longer needed should be deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

Your questions or deletion requests

You can contact kronatrix.ai@gmail.com if you want to ask what enquiry information KRONATRIX holds about you, correct it, or request deletion where applicable.

External websites

This website links to client websites, social networks, Google, KRONATRIX properties and other external services. Those websites operate under their own privacy practices.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website, forms, analytics configuration or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
