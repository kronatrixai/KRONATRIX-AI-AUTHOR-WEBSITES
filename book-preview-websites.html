What is a book preview website?

A dedicated website for one book that lets readers experience less than 10% of the book online and then gives them a direct next step: buy the full book, follow the author or join the author's email list.

Personalised to the book

The visual language, hierarchy and reader journey are built around the individual title rather than a generic catalogue.

Preview before purchase

A reader can begin reading on the site without needing to understand the whole author brand first.

Clear next action

The preview is connected to purchase, author-follow and email-list actions so attention has somewhere useful to go.
